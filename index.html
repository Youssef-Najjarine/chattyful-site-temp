What ChattyFul is

ChattyFul is a peer-support chat app. When you need to talk, it matches you in real time with someone offering to listen and help. When you have room to give, you can be that someone for another person. Around the matching sits everything you'd expect from a modern chat app: direct messages, group conversations, friends, and profiles.

The ChattyFul app for iOS is coming soon, with Android to follow.

Messages from ChattyFul

Text messages: when you create a ChattyFul account with your phone number or update it later, we send a one-time verification code by SMS to that number. Codes arrive only when you request them. We do not send marketing texts. Message and data rates may apply.

Email: account emails such as verification codes, password resets, and security alerts come from noreply@chattyful.com.
