Messages from ChattyFul

We only message you about your account. Here's exactly what to expect.

Text messages

When you create a ChattyFul account with your phone number, change that number, or reset your password, we send a one-time code by SMS to that number. We also text you a security alert if your number is changed. Codes arrive only when you ask for them, and we never send marketing texts. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Email

Account emails such as verification codes, password resets, and security alerts come from noreply@chattyful.com. If something about your account changes, that's where we'll tell you.

The full detail lives in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
