2. What ChattyFul is

ChattyFul is a peer-support chat app. It connects people who want to talk with people who are willing to listen. Conversations happen between ordinary people.

The people you talk to on ChattyFul are not therapists, counsellors, doctors, social workers, or crisis responders, and we do not vet, train, license, certify, or supervise them. Nothing said on ChattyFul is medical, psychological, or professional advice. ChattyFul is not a substitute for professional care, and it is not a crisis service.

3. Crisis situations and emergencies

ChattyFul is not an emergency service and is not monitored for emergencies. Nobody is watching conversations in real time, and there is no guarantee that anyone will see a message quickly or at all.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number right away. In the United States that is 911. If you are having thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or you are worried about someone who is, you can reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988 in the United States, at any hour.

Please do not rely on ChattyFul for urgent help. Reach a person or service who can actually respond.
